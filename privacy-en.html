This policy explains what information the mobile game Slidoor ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by , an individual developer ("we").

1. Summary

  • You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, address or phone number.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, stars, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, and AppLovin to show ads. These services receive limited technical information, described below.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings are saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

3. Information collected by services in the game

ServiceInformation / Purpose
Firebase Analytics (Google) App instance ID, device model, operating system, language, country (estimated from IP address), and game events such as levels started and finished, boosters used, ads watched and items bought (product and price).
Purpose: understand which levels are too hard, improve the game, measure the business.
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash reports, device state at the time of a crash, installation ID, the level being played.
Purpose: find and fix crashes.
Firebase Remote Config and Installations (Google) Installation ID, app version, language, country.
Purpose: adjust game settings (for example rewards or difficulty) without an app update.
AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking; Google advertising ID on Android according to your device settings), IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads.
Purpose: show ads, measure them, limit how often they appear, and prevent fraud.
Apple App Store / Google Play Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made.

Provider policies: Firebase, Google, AppLovin, Apple.

4. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before personalised ads are used. The form appears after your first win, not when you first open the game.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) after your first win. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Remove Ads: the Remove Ads purchase turns off ads between levels. Reward videos stay available only when you choose to watch them.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: according to the policies of AppLovin and its partners.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information), you may ask to access or delete information about you, or object to its use. Google and AppLovin may process information in the United States and other countries, with safeguards described in their policies.
